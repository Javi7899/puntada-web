In one sentence: Puntada collects no personal data, has no accounts, has no analytics and sends nothing to any server of mine, because there is no server of mine.

What is stored and where

Puntada stores on your device what it needs to work: which lessons you have done, your practice minutes, the level of your techniques, the state of your review questions, your notes, your body measurements if you choose to enter them and your log of makes. That information never leaves your device.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They do not go through any server.

Purchases

The full course purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Puntada does not receive your payment details.

What there is not

  • No user accounts or sign-up.
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking.
  • No third-party components or libraries.
  • No use of the camera, microphone or location.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Crash reports

If you have "Share With App Developers" turned on on your device, Apple may send me anonymous technical reports of crashes. Apple handles them and you can turn them off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Puntada collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will show it.
