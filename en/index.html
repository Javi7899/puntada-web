Who it is for

For people who have never sewn and for those who know a little and want to put it in order: mending, the machine, patterns and garments. No account, works offline.

What is inside

  • An ordered two-year course, with a new lesson every week and a short session every day.
  • Over 200 illustrations that build themselves: every stitch seen from the front, the back and inside the fold.
  • Projects you will use (tote bag, apron, cushion, skirt, T-shirt, lined jacket) with their cutting layouts.
  • Tools: your measurements and size, needle and thread for each fabric, circle-skirt, curtain and bias calculators.
  • Workroom mode: the steps one at a time, big, with the screen kept on while you sew.

Price

The first module (about a month) is free. The rest of the course unlocks with a single payment, no subscription, and works with Family Sharing.

Help

Puntada is a two-year sewing course for iPhone and iPad. It takes you from threading your first needle to sewing your own clothes, with one lesson a week and a short practice session every day.

Frequently asked questions

What is free and what is paid?

The first two modules of the course (eight lessons, about two months) are free, with the daily plan, review, animated stitches and the measurement, needle and conversion tools. The full course unlocks with a single payment. It is not a subscription: you pay once and it is yours for good, on all your devices with the same Apple Account, and it can be shared with Family Sharing.

I bought the course and it does not show as unlocked

Open Settings (the gear on the Today tab) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are using the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one from Settings › Request a Refund in the app, or at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I go faster than one lesson a week?

Yes. In Settings › Course pace you can choose two lessons a week or no limit. And from the Course tab you can open any lesson whenever you like.

Do I need a sewing machine?

Not to start: the first term is by hand. The machine arrives in the second, with a lesson on what to look for if you are buying one.

What materials do I need?

To start, a basic sewing kit: needles, thread, pins, fabric scissors, a tape measure and a few cotton scraps. Each lesson says at the start what it needs.
